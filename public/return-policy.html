Return Policy

At ZealWear, we want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase. If you're not happy with your purchase for any reason, you may return it in accordance with the following guidelines:

  1. Eligibility for Returns:

    • You may return items within 7 days of the date of delivery.
    • To be eligible for a return, the item must be in its original condition, unused, and with all tags and packaging intact.

  2. How to Initiate a Return:

    • To initiate a return, please contact our customer support team at zeal@relaxzeal.com or +91 7057923102.
    • Provide your order number and a detailed reason for the return.
    • Our customer support team will guide you through the return process and provide you with a return authorization if applicable.

  3. Return Shipping:

    • You are responsible for shipping the item back to us.
    • Please pack the item securely to prevent damage during transit.
    • We recommend using a trackable and insured shipping method.
    • Shipping costs for returns are your responsibility unless the return is due to our error or a defective product.

  4. Inspection and Processing:

    • Once we receive the returned item, our team will inspect it to ensure it meets the return eligibility criteria.
    • If the return is approved, we will process your refund or exchange within 7 business days.
    • Refunds will be issued to the original payment method used for the purchase.

  5. Refund and Exchange Options:

    • You can choose to receive a refund to your original payment method or store credit.
    • If you prefer an exchange, we will send the replacement item as soon as the return is processed.
    • Please note that some items may not be eligible for exchanges due to availability.

  6. Non-Returnable Items:

    Certain items are not eligible for return, including:

    • Items marked as final sale.
    • Personalized or custom-made products.
    • Undergarments and swimwear (for hygiene reasons).

  7. Damage or Defective Items:

    • If you receive a damaged or defective item, please contact us immediately.
    • We will arrange for a replacement or provide further instructions on resolving the issue.

  8. Contact Information:

    For any questions or concerns regarding our return policy, please reach out to us at zeal@relaxzeal.com or +91 7057923102.
